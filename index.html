Meet the duo

DSHunt and Vix Malone are two mates who have been playing together for five years around the South East. Jamnesia is where they let loose their passion for dance music, reworking classic house, vocal house and Ibiza anthems as an acoustic duo.

DSHunt

Acoustic guitar, backing vocals, percussion and piano. A songwriter whose original tracks often feature Vix on vocals.

Vix Malone

Vocals. DSHunt's long-time friend, the singer on many of his original tracks, and the voice that fills the dance floor.
